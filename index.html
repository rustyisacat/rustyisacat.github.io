What I actually do

I'm a hobbyist systems tinkerer. I run my own Hyprland desktop tuned to the last pixel, keep a small homelab of always-on servers, and write the tools I wish existed instead of settling for the closest commercial product. Most of what's below started as "I wish I had a thing that ..." and ended as a real, tested, working release. I care a lot about things actually working end-to-end, verified on real hardware, not just assumed correct, and about keeping my own data on my own machines.
